# Code of conduct

This project is a place to do science and write software together. The rules
are short.

**Expected.** Be civil and be precise. Assume the other person is competent
and acting in good faith, including when they are wrong. Criticise the code,
the method or the result, never the person. English is a second language for
many people here, including the maintainer, so read past awkward phrasing to
the argument underneath, and ask if something is unclear rather than assuming
the worst reading.

**Not accepted.** Personal attacks and insults; harassment of any kind;
demeaning comments about someone's background, nationality, gender, religion,
age or ability; deliberately derailing discussions; publishing anyone's
private information.

**Scope.** This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
space belonging to the project, and to private contact that arises from them.

**If something goes wrong.** Report it to the maintainer privately through
the contact details on his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/IakOBiaN).
Reports are handled discreetly. Depending on what happened, the response
ranges from a private word to blocking an account and removing its content.
GitHub's own [abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) is
always available as well, and is the right route if the maintainer is the
problem.
